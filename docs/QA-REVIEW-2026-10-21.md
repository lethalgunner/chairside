# QA review: Pilot 0 build before the parallel run (21 Oct 2026)

**Build:** `pilot0` @ `87a8142` (the commit deployed to chairside-pi.vercel.app), run locally as a production build (`next build` + `next start`) on Postgres 16 with the synthetic demo org. Not run against the live Mumbai deployment.
**Reviewer:** chairside-qa. **For:** CPO triage. Review only; no product code changed.

## Summary
1. Billing is correct: every bill matched the hand calculation, double-clicks and a slow network produced exactly one document, a closed day blocks billing, and axe found no violations in 11 scripted scans at 1366 px and 390 px, plus the e2e sweep of 11 screens.
2. Correcting mistakes is the weak point. A recorded payment can never be reversed, a paid bill cannot be cancelled, and front-desk staff cannot cancel any bill and get no hint who can (Q7, Q9).
3. The comparison with the old system is only half built. Day close takes the old system's collected amount but never shows the difference, and the owner's view shows bill counts only (Q10). That comparison is the main thing the pilot is measuring.
4. Q5 came back: 2 of 26 e2e tests in the first run stuck on "Saving…" even though the patients were saved. It did not recur in 3 later runs or 41 scripted registrations. The likely cause is in the client navigation (Q8).
5. Three input traps can produce a wrong bill or lost work: the quantity field turns 2 into 12 (Q11), a network drop wipes the bill being built (Q12), and phone search misses numbers typed with +91 or a leading 0 (Q14).

## Recommend for the CPO (ranked for the 26 Oct start)
1. **Q7: a way to undo a payment** (owner-only cancel with reason). Without it, one typo spoils that branch's day comparison.
2. **Q10: show the money difference against the old system** on day close and the owner view, with billed ₹ and collected by mode. This is the pilot's success metric.
3. **Q9: decide who cancels bills at Kanpur**, and at least tell the desk who to ask.
4. **Q8 (Q5) and Q12: keep forms alive.** Fix the navigation after registration, and keep the form open when the network drops. Both are small fixes.
5. **Q11: the quantity field.** Small fix, and it prevents wrong bills.
6. **Q14: search by +91 or a leading 0.** Small fix, and it prevents duplicates.
7. **Q18: the phone layout for the owner view and Bills list**, since Dr Anshul's evening check is likely on a phone.
8. **Q19: a past-day view and a "Today" filter on Bills** for the daily side-by-side.
9. Then Q15, Q16, Q17, Q20 and Q21 (desk speed and copy), and Q13.

For the run itself, until Q10 ships: ask staff to note the old system's **billed total and collected by mode** at close, and have QA keep the comparison and the friction log (time · who · screen · what happened · S1–S4) in a shared sheet.
